    Objective
    Help GPs bring patients back for the second shingles dose, and lift two-dose completion in pilot areas.
    Audience
    186 GPs in the pilot regions who raised the topic or opened the second-dose guide. All have opted in to personalisation.
    Format
    One-page patient card in large print (Spanish), with a space for the second-dose date and a QR to the Ministry of Health’s patient Q&A.
    Key messages · from the approved claims library
    1. The schedule is two doses.Approved · C-HZ-012
    2. The second dose is given 2 months after the first.Approved · C-HZ-015
    3. Most patients just need a reminder.New wording · needs MLR review
    Channels
    Rep hand-over at 28 upcoming visits · GSKGO briefing for 158 GPs · no email
    How we’ll know it worked
    Two-dose completion at 65 in pilot areas vs matched control (Ministry data) · relevance ≥ 4/5 · fewer repeat questions within 60 days

    Drafted in 1 min 52 s. A brief like this usually takes days of back-and-forth (illustrative).
